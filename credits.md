## Voices

All spoken lines are text-to-speech renders by ingo in a voice cloned from a LibriVox reader of the
"LibriVox Dialect and Accent Collection Vol. 1" (https://archive.org/details/dialect_accent_0909_librivox), which is in
the **public domain**.

## Tank anthem

`sound/god-save-the-king.ogg` is a cut of England fans singing "God Save the King" at MetLife Stadium (FIFA World Cup
2026), recorded and originally posted on Twitter/X by Phil Clarke, released without restrictions and used with his permission; source video:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=coo1dzoN8SE. Trimmed to one verse, mono, loudness-normalised.

## Truthbomb line

`sound/truthbomb.ogg` is Michael Caine's line "You're only supposed to blow the bloody doors off!" from
_The Italian Job_ (1969), a 3-second quotation used under fair use (parody). Source clip:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7_PX1cVuaVA (Movieclips), 20-25 s, silence trimmed.

## Victory sound

`sound/victory.ogg` is a 26.5-second excerpt of the "Fuck This Shit I'm Out" meme song, used under fair use
(parody). Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9wO5TnYaJ4o, 0:50 to the silence at 1:16.

## Icons

The item, technology and achievement icons are renders by ingo of free 3D models, all **modified** (recoloured,
combined or retextured). Every model, its author, source link and licence (CC0, CC BY 3.0 or CC BY 4.0) is listed in
[`graphics/icons/ICON_CREDITS_ingo.txt`](graphics/icons/ICON_CREDITS_ingo.txt), which ships with the mod.
